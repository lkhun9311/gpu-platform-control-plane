\[
U_l(X,Y)=\ln(T^+_{X,l}/T^-_{Y,l}),\qquad
L_l(X,Y)=-U_l(Y,X),
\]

where \(T^+\) is scheduled-start upper p99 and \(T^-\) arrival-start lower p99, with uncertain observations bounded as the current scorer does. These are measurement bounds, not confidence intervals.

\[
K_l(X,Y):
\quad c_{X,l}\ge c_{Y,l}-0.01,\quad
\bar p_{X,l}\ge0.97\bar p_{Y,l},\quad
\bar q_{X,l}\ge0.97\bar q_{Y,l}.
\]

Here c is pooled completion; the bars are arithmetic means of the three block fractions. Define:

\[
W_l(X,Y)=\operatorname{Adm}(X,l)\land K_l(X,Y)

\]

\[
N_l(X,Y)=\operatorname{Adm}(X,l)\land K_l(X,Y)
          \land U_l(X,Y)\le\ln(1.15).
\]
