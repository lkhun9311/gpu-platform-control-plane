C3 main study: the purchase gate, frozen 2026-10-25 before it was run

Committed before predict-main-gate.py was run on the frozen spacings, so neither the rules nor the pace can move after its result.
The C3 calibration page fixes the gate's shape ("What comes after, not registered here"): replay seeds 882, 892 and 893 once, at the calibration card's pace and S; if it predicts hold-cap inadmissible anywhere, or the main comparison unreachable, the study stops and no seed is swapped.
This file makes that shape exact, taking astra's resolution of the pace (astra-independent-draft.txt, "Purchase gate") and the conditions of its primary verdicts 2 to 6.

Not blind, said before it is run: predict-main-derived-<pace>.json in data/2026-10-24-c3-headroom/ already replayed these seeds, with spacings that moved with the pace, and were read while the calibration page was written.
At pace 1.0275 those spacings were 1,672.9, 1,208.3 and 1,818.4 ms, not the frozen ones below.

Inputs, all fixed:
- traces: the 9 seed-882, -892 and -893 traces at 0.375/s, under the checksums in data/2026-10-24-c3-headroom/trace-checksums.txt;
- the model: simulate.py's step model fitted on pilots A and B, as every earlier replay;
- arms: off; cap-only (cap 384); hold-cap (hold-one-prefill, cap 384); fixed-S at 1,680.4 ms at every length (cap 384); size-aware at 1,176.9, 1,680.4 and 1,830.8 ms (cap 384);
- nominal pace 1.0275: of the replay grid's paces, the one whose seed-891 hold-cap median at ref (1,588.27 ms, predict-cal891-anchor1740-1.0275.json) lies nearest the card's 1,595.40 ms.

Control before the gate: the driver run at 1.0275 with the earlier derived spacings (1672.9, 1208.3, 1818.4) must reproduce predict-main-derived-1.0275.json's pooled p99 and hold-cap prefill median at every length.
If it does not, the driver is not the replay the calibration page cited, and the gate is not run.

The gate, on the replay's point estimates at the nominal pace; every condition must hold.
Pooled p99 is over the three seeds' premium requests, per arm and length, never across lengths. d is the replay's hold-cap prefill median, first token less engagement, per seed.
G1 hold-cap passes the four modelled contender limits (completion, completion p50 against off's, completion p95, no hold refusal) at every length on every seed.
G2 hold-cap protective: pooled p99(hold-cap) <= 0.85 x pooled p99(off) at every length.
G3 the challenge, on every seed: d(ref) <= 1,680.4 ms, d(long) > 1,680.4 ms, and d(long) > 1.05 x d(ref).
   The 1.05 condition is back because S is frozen now and no longer recomputed from each card's reference (astra, risk 6).
G4 the reference comparison reachable: fixed-S passes the four limits at ref on every seed; pooled p99(fixed-S) <= 0.85 x pooled p99(off) at ref; and neither of hold-cap and fixed-S is 15% below the other at ref.
G5 the long separation and the interaction: at long, fixed-S fails a modelled limit on some seed, or pooled p99(hold-cap) <= 0.85 x pooled p99(fixed-S);
   and ln(p99 H/F at long) - ln(p99 H/F at ref) <= ln 0.85, where an infinite fixed-S p99 against a finite hold-cap p99 counts as satisfying it and an infinite hold-cap p99 as failing it.

Nothing is required of size-aware or cap-only: either outcome of theirs answers the main study's secondary questions.

What the gate may decide: stop the purchase. It may not swap a seed, move the pace, the rates or a spacing, or be re-run at another pace to pass.
What a pass means: the modelled prerequisites of the main study's verdicts 2 to 6 are reachable at the nominal pace. It does not predict the verdict:
preemption, shared-window work and premium gaps are not modelled; the replay's d differs from the scorer's in definition and population; and the replay has understated the contender's cost by 3% to 7% against every card measured.

Sensitivity, published and not deciding: the same run at paces 1.000, 1.0205 and 1.070.
