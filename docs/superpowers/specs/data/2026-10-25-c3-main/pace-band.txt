Sensitivity only. The gate (gate.txt) passed at its frozen pace, 1.0275, and nothing here decides or re-runs it.
It answers one question before any build: how likely is a new card to land outside the paces where the replay predicts the main comparison reachable?

Corrected after astra's cold attack of the registration (astra-attack.txt, finding 3).
The first version of this file shifted every card's pace by -0.0031, on the assumption that the gate's nominal 1.0275 was the calibration card's matched pace. It is not: it is the replay grid's point nearest that card.
Matched exactly (gen891-pace.py), the generated seed-891 ref trace reaches the card's 1,595.40 ms between 1.031 (1,593.99) and 1.032 (1,596.44), at about 1.0316; the card's own archived trace gives 1.0306 (card-pace.jsonl).

The step-time ratio the pace tool prints (step-time-pace-all-cards.txt, simulate.py pace) is a different quantity: C3's ref cell is 1.0181 by it and 1.0306 by d. The gate's pace is the d one.

What it says.
- The lower edge sits 0.023 below every long cell measured; no card has run near it.
- The upper edge sits 0.0022 above the slowest ref cell measured, which is C3's own, and 0.0012 above its exact match on the generated trace. C3 is the only card at this load (0.375 contenders a second).
- The cells are not independent draws: the seven at 0.5/s come from three cards, three cells each from two of them and one from the third. With that said, and assuming a normal spread of sd 0.0033 per cell, a ref cell above the edge is under 1% per cell if new cards run like the 0.5/s ones (mean 1.0235), and about one in four per cell if they run like C3's card (mean 1.0306). These are illustrations of the margin, not probabilities a reader should rely on.
- So the calibration card itself, replayed on the main study's seeds, sits at the edge where the reference tie breaks toward hold-cap, rule 5b of the registration.
- These are the replay's point estimates. The main study decides on the card's measured p99s with crossed bounds, and the replay has understated the contender's cost by 3% to 7%.
- An earlier statement in conversation, that cards ran 3% to 7% slower than the model "toward the 1.070 side", confused the contender-cost error with pace. By d, no ref cell has run above 1.0306.

Not done: a pace per length in the gate's replay. On both cards that ran all three lengths, the long cell's pace exceeds the ref cell's (by 0.0059 and 0.0016), which widens the G3 margin, and the short cell's is lower.
