7. **The fixed-spacing semantics are stated more strongly than the implementation guarantees.**

   The page says an expiring waiter gets no slot. In the gateway, `release()` advances the clock before the waiting goroutine returns. If timeout/cancellation and its turn are both ready, `Reserve()` can return a refusal after the clock advanced. `leave()` explicitly documents that this slot remains spent. This matters because the release-deviation witness measures gaps between recorded admissions, excluding the refused request that consumed the slot.
