4. **Major — “No hot backend” can pass by comparing infinity with infinity.**

6. **Major — The 19-request escape threshold is not derived from the stated scheduling model.**  
   **Evidence:** [Lines 44–49](/home/lkhun9311/workspace/gpu-platform-control-plane/.claude/worktrees/agent-a279fe81eee6dfa00/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-prefix-aware-routing-design.md:44) infer that each outstanding request costs at most one step because the *new hit request* fits in one step. Speculative index insertion also does not establish that a purported hit has finished prefilling.    **Fix:** Treat the rule as an explicitly uncalibrated heuristic, or derive it from remaining work and measured scheduling behavior. Record matched-prefix estimates, outstanding counts and escape decisions if the assumption is meant to be tested.
